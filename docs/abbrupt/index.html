About this trial

Need to know: amiodarone vs. a beta-blocker for new-onset atrial fibrillation (NOAF) in critically ill adults — both drugs are used routinely but there's no clear evidence which gives the best outcome.
  • Multicentre, open-label RCT — adults aged ≥16 with new-onset AF in critical illness
  • Randomised to amiodarone or a beta-blocker (agent/dose chosen by the treating clinician)
  • Treatment continues until sinus rhythm has been maintained for 24 hours
  • Primary outcome: 90-day mortality
  • Target 2,560 participants (1,280/arm) across England, Scotland, Wales & Northern Ireland
  • Recruiting since May 2024; study completion expected 31 Dec 2026
  • Note: the ISRCTN registry currently shows an outdated “no longer recruiting” status — treat BCTU as the current source
