Step 1 of 2 — Inclusion criteria

1 Adult

Inclusion criterion 1: aged ≥16 years

2 Monitored in an area of higher care

Inclusion criterion 2: monitored in an area of higher care with the ability to deliver the interventions (amiodarone or beta-blockade)

3 New-onset AF with a clinical indication to treat

Inclusion criterion 3: onset of NOAF, or a new episode of AF, during the acute illness (A&E, deterioration on ward, after surgery), where there is a clinical indication to treat with amiodarone or beta-blockers as determined by the attending clinician/ACCP or equivalent

4 Usual electrolyte management

Inclusion criterion 4: usual electrolyte management with potassium and magnesium has taken place according to site practice

This is expected to happen, not just be checked — ensure usual K+/Mg2+ replacement per site practice is done before screening potassium in Step 2. An earlier low reading does not exclude the patient if potassium is now in range.

Step 2 of 2 — Exclusion criteria
Confirmation of eligibility is a medical decision. Per protocol section 4.3, it may be confirmed by a medically qualified doctor, or by an ACCP/equivalent (ANP, CNS) working under the supervision of a medically qualified doctor — provided they are trained on ABBRUPT procedures and delegated the task by the PI.

Serum potassium

Drives exclusion criterion 3 below.

Point-of-care or laboratory measurement, taken after usual K+/Mg2+ replacement (inclusion criterion 4) — excludes if <4 mmol/L. Use the current value: an earlier low reading does not exclude if potassium is now in range.
