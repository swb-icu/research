About this trial

RELEASE is a multicentre, pragmatic, randomised controlled trial comparing early Airway Pressure Release Ventilation (APRV) with conventional lung-protective invasive mechanical ventilation (IMV) in adults with moderate–severe acute hypoxaemic respiratory failure (AHRF). APRV uses a longer inspiration time with a brief expiration, which may reduce ventilator-induced lung injury by maintaining lung volume and limiting excessive stretch and collapse.

The primary outcome is duration of invasive mechanical ventilation (time from randomisation to first successful unassisted breathing, or death). The trial aims to recruit 710 participants across UK sites; recruitment began February 2025 and is scheduled to continue to May 2027.
