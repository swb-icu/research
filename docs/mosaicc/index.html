About this trial

MOSAICC (Multicentre evaluation Of Sodium bicarbonate in Acute kidney Injury in Critical Care) is a multicentre, open-label, randomised controlled trial evaluating the clinical and cost-effectiveness of intravenous sodium bicarbonate for critically ill adults with metabolic acidosis and acute kidney injury (AKI). Around half of the ~184,000 adults admitted to UK critical care each year develop AKI, and the resulting acidosis carries a very poor prognosis (90-day mortality around 59%). Kidney replacement therapy is the current mainstay treatment, but it is invasive and resource-intensive; sodium bicarbonate is a cheap, accessible alternative with limited high-quality evidence behind it.

Eligible patients are randomised to IV sodium bicarbonate or no sodium bicarbonate, with follow-up at 28 days, 90 days, and 1 year. The primary outcome is all-cause mortality at 90 days; cost-effectiveness (incremental cost, QALYs, net monetary benefit) is assessed alongside. The trial aims to recruit 2,250 patients from 60 NHS critical care units across the UK, and is open nationally — this site is still in set-up and not yet recruiting locally.
