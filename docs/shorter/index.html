About this trial

Need to know: is a fixed 5-day antibiotic course as safe as standard duration (~9 days) for adults with sepsis in critical care?
  • Pragmatic, open-label, multicentre, parallel-arm RCT with an internal pilot and embedded process evaluation
  • Fixed 5-day antibiotic course vs. standard-of-care duration (typically ~9 days)
  • Choice/combination of antibiotics stays with the treating clinical team; extra courses still allowed if clinically needed
  • Co-primary outcomes: 28-day all-cause mortality (non-inferiority) and total antibiotic days at 28 days (superiority)
  • Target 2,244 participants across up to 50 UK critical care units
  • Screening window: can be randomised during antibiotic treatment days 1–4 of the index sepsis course
