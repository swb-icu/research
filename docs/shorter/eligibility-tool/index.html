Step 1 of 2 — Inclusion criteria

1 Adult, in critical care, for sepsis

Inclusion criterion 1: adult patients, aged ≥18, treated within a critical care setting (ICU or HDU) for suspected or confirmed sepsis due to either community- or hospital-acquired infections

2 New/worsening organ dysfunction from infection

Inclusion criterion 2: evidence of new or worsening acute organ dysfunction resulting from suspected or confirmed infection (e.g. the treatment or monitoring of an organ dysfunction)

3 Within antibiotic day 1–4

Inclusion criterion 3: antibiotics initiated for suspected or confirmed sepsis, and able to be randomised within 4 days of the initiation of this index course

Day 1 = the day the current course of antibiotics for this sepsis episode started. Eligible for screening/randomisation on days 1–4 of that course only. If the index course was later reset for a blood-culture-proven resistant organism (protocol 3.3.1), count from the reset date.
