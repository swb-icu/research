Method. This back-calculates serum creatinine from the four-variable MDRD equation, assuming a fixed eGFR of 75 mL/min/1.73m² — the AKIN/KDIGO convention for estimating a baseline creatinine when no genuine pre-illness value is available, so that a subsequent rise can be staged against something.

step = 75 / (sex_factor × age-0.203 × 175), where sex_factor = 0.742 for female, 1 for male.
baseline Cr (µmol/L) = step(1 / -1.154) × 88.4 + 3.056

Known limitations, carried over from the source spreadsheet this was built from: the eGFR of 75 is an assumed convention, not a measurement — it will misrepresent true baseline in patients with genuine pre-existing CKD; no ethnicity coefficient is applied (the standard four-variable MDRD equation includes a ×1.212 factor for Black patients, omitted here); and the +3.056 offset is carried over from the original spreadsheet with its provenance unconfirmed (likely an assay/calibration correction). Verify against your local method before relying on this for a clinical or trial-eligibility decision.
