MOSAICC Eligibility Aid

Multicentre evaluation Of Sodium bicarbonate in Acute kidney Injury in Critical Care — bedside reference tool

Support aid only — not a substitute for the trial Screening Log or PI sign-off. This tool implements the inclusion/exclusion criteria from MOSAICC Protocol v6.0 (20FEB2025) as written. It does not connect to the trial database, does not record or transmit any data, and does not replace clinical judgement. Confirm any eligibility decision against the current approved protocol version and discuss with your PI/research team before enrolment. Baseline creatinine must be entered manually — the protocol's own "provided calculator" for estimating baseline creatinine is not yet integrated into this tool.
